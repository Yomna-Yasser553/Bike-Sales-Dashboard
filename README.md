
# 📊 Bike Sales Dashboard & Analysis

An interactive Excel Sales Dashboard analyzing customer demographics, purchasing patterns, and sales performance with dual-theme (Dark/Light) visual design.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing bike sales data to extract actionable business insights. The interactive dashboard allows stakeholders to explore key metrics such as total revenue, profit margins, and sales performance across different regions and customer age groups.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* Microsoft Excel:
  * Power Query: Data cleaning, transformation, and custom column creation.
  * Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts: Data aggregation and dynamic chart creation.
  * Advanced Formulas: XLOOKUP, KPI measures, and calculated fields.
  * Interactive Elements: Custom Slicers, Timeline filters, and VBA theme toggle logic.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Features
* Overall Margin (%): Real-time margin percentage tracking (~38%).
* Total Revenue & Profit: Dynamic KPI cards tracking total revenue ($85.2M) and profit ($32.2M).
* Demographic Analysis: Profit breakdowns by customer age groups (Adults, Seniors, Young Adults, Youth).
* Geographic Performance: Revenue distributions across major countries and top-performing states.
* Sales Trends: Monthly sales and profit trend charts for seasonality analysis.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* Top Demographic: Adults (35-64) generate the highest proportion of total profit.
* Best Performing Category: Bikes drive the majority of overall revenue compared to Accessories and Clothing.
* Geographic Trends: California and England lead as top revenue-generating regions.
